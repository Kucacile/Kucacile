PRECISE TRAVEL . STRATEGIC SOLUTIONS

Harvest Corporate Travel is a premier Travel Management Company specializing in crafting travel management solutions to SMMEs and Large corporations. This Female Owned business offers more than 20 years of knowledge and experience in planning and booking travel arrangements such as flights, accommodations, tours, and transportation as well as budget and cost control, policy compliance and destination expertise.

01 Technology Driven Efficiency

Harvest Corporate Travel uses the latest GDS platforms, combining real-time data, streamlined booking, and automated policy compliance. By leveraging integrated platforms, we deliver faster, more accurate, and efficient travel management.

02 Bespoke Services

At Harvest Corporate Travel, personalised travel plans cater to each traveller’s preferences, booking history, and loyalty programs. We advise on options that align with both the traveller's needs and the company policy.

03 Data-Driven Decision Making

Harvest Corporate Travel provides data and reporting to optimise your travel program—spotting spending trends, enabling smart recommendations, and reducing costs in line with your business goals.

04 Policy Management

Harvest Corporate Travel enforces real‑time booking compliance through integrated platforms—instantly applying and updating travel policies to prevent non‑compliant or overspend bookings.
