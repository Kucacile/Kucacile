Our Services

Harvest Corporate Travel focuses on providing the following services to all travelers travelling on behalf of the company, locally and internationally. Our Operational Service

Focuses on providing the following services to all travelers travelling on behalf of the company, locally and internationally. This will include employees and contractors, consultants and clients where the agreement is that the company is responsible for the arrangement and cost of travel.

Reservations

Planning, booking, confirming and amending of accommodation with any establishment (hotel group, private hotel, guest house or Bed & Breakfast) in accordance with the company’s travel policy. Negotiating discounts on standard rates or reduced rates with all available hotel groups, private hotels and or other accommodation providers.

Air Travel

Arranging and amending air travel in accordance with the company’s travel policy. Keeping abreast of carrier schedule changes as well as all other alterations and new conditions affecting travel and make appropriate adjustments for any changes in flight schedules.

Car Rental & Shuttle Service

Booking vehicles in line with car rental companies and amending any confirmed bookings if necessary. Booking transport, including a driver, with shuttle service/car rental companies. Ensure that relevant information is shared with travelers regarding rental vehicles, like e-tolls, refueling, keys, rental agreements, damages and accidents, etc.

After Hours and Emergency Services

A consultant or a team of consultants will assist Travelers with after hours and emergency reservations and changes to travel plans. Giving you the best 24hrs of great customer services.

01 Technology Driven Efficiency

Harvest Corporate Travel uses Amadeus and TBOholidays GDS platforms, combining real-time data, streamlined booking, and automated policy compliance. By leveraging integrated platforms, we deliver faster, more accurate, and efficient travel management.

02 Bespoke Services

At Harvest Corporate Travel, personalised travel plans cater to each traveller’s preferences, booking history, and loyalty programs. We advise on options that align with both the traveller's needs and the company policy.

03 Data-Driven Decision Making

Harvest Corporate Travel provides data and reporting to optimise your travel program—spotting spending trends, enabling smart recommendations, and reducing costs in line with your business goals.

04 Policies and Compliance

Harvest Corporate Travel enforces real‑time booking compliance through integrated platforms—instantly applying and updating travel policies to prevent non‑compliant or overspend bookings.
