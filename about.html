About Us

Harvest Corporate Travel

Harvest Corporate Travel aims to be a placeholder in the corporate landscape and is crafted to meet the needs and core functions of modern businesses.

Our purpose at Harvest Corporate Travel is to Grow by growing others by creating strategic solutions that promote industry leadership. As a women only, youth empowered Company, Harvest Corporate Travel ensures young people are not just participants but are actively involved in decision-making, leadership and overall direction of the company.

As a Level 1 BBBEE contributor and member of South African Council of Business Women, our business strategies reflect our priority to preserve cultural heritage, strengthen community resilience, and contribute to a more inclusive and equitable society.

Technology

We leverage advanced technology to enhance every step of the travel management process. Integrated platforms allow us to access real-time travel data, streamline bookings, and enforce policy compliance. By operating within this digital network, we are able to offer more accurate and efficient solutions.

Bespoke Services

Customized travel plans ensure specific needs and preferences of individual travelers are met. At Harvest Corporate Travel we ensure personalized support, with access to each traveler’s preferences, booking history, and loyalty programs, this enables us to advise on the best options that suit both the traveler’s needs and the company’s travel policy.

Analytics

Access to information to make core decisions is prime. Harvest Corporate Travel provides a wealth of data and reporting to assist businesses optimize business travel programs. These analytics allow us to identify patterns in travel spending, make informed recommendations, and suggest cost-saving measures that align with the company’s objectives.

Policies / Compliance

Harvest Corporate Travel assists businesses to enforce travel policies in real time. through existing platforms. We are able to set and update travel guidelines and guarantee that bookings comply with these rules, helping to prevent overspending or non-compliant bookings
